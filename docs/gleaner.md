# mod-temucraft-gleaner (part of mod-temucraft-aoe-loot)

**Gleaner-3000**, a little loot bot. Use the item to call it (use it again to send it home). While it follows you
and you play alone in the open world, whatever a monster you or your pet kill drops goes straight into your bags:
gold and items. Quest items, items you aren't allowed to loot and anything that doesn't fit stay on the corpse.

- Not in groups or dungeons and raids by default, so group loot rules stay fair (both are settings).
- It saves time, it doesn't give anything extra: the same loot you would pick up yourself.
- Item 610105, sold on the Temucraft Curator's Gameplay list for 250 tokens. Bot creature 608970 (the stock mechanical
  squirrel look, made bigger).

Install: put the folder in `modules/`, re-run CMake, build, copy the conf file.

Not tested in game yet.
